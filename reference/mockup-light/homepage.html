#How I Work

From search data to a site people use

The same four steps run through every project, from a full website build to a single landing page.

Research

Search queries, Search Console data and competitor research show what users actually ask, where they get stuck, and where the gaps are.

Structure

Information architecture, sitemaps and page templates built around those questions, with schema, hreflang and speed built in.

Journey

Clear paths from first visit to lead: landing pages, forms and WhatsApp flows, fed by campaigns on Meta, Google and TikTok.

Measure

GSC and GA4 reporting, lead-quality tracking, and Claude-assisted workflows that automate the repetitive work so time goes to the next improvement.

#Questions

What I can do for you

The questions I get asked most.

What do you actually do?

I design and build websites and conversion journeys around real user demand. My background is technical SEO and performance marketing, so I start from data (what people search for, where they drop off) and then design the structure, pages and funnels that answer it. That's four years across Indonesia, the Philippines and the US.

Are you a UX designer or a marketer?

Both, and that's the point. Most designers never see the search and campaign data, and most marketers don't shape the site. I do both, so my design decisions come with numbers: +600% organic traffic MoM at MAKA Motors, 13× organic clicks at GNET, and $160K+ in paid budget managed across Meta, Google, TikTok and YouTube.

Which markets do you work in?

Indonesia, the Philippines and the US. I've handled dual-market ID + PH SEO with hreflang and schema, and run full-funnel performance for a US-market SaaS, so I adapt structure and messaging to each market.

Do you build websites too?

Yes. I've shipped four end-to-end website builds covering structure, on-page SEO, CMS and Core Web Vitals, not just the marketing layer on top. This site is built in Framer.

How do you use AI in your work?

I build Claude-assisted workflows that automate the repetitive parts of the job: Search Console reporting, site audits and pre-publish checks. That way my time goes to research and design decisions.

What kind of roles are you open to?

Full-time, freelance and consulting: UX roles with a growth focus, web or growth lead roles, and hybrid technical-marketing roles. I'm based in Tangerang, Indonesia, and remote-friendly.
