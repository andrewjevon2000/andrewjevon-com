#UX Case Study · Website Revamp

Designing for a category nobody was searching for

MAKA Motors' website revamp, structured around the questions EV buyers actually ask.

#PeriodJun 2024 – Sep 2025
#RoleSEO & IA lead, revamp
#MarketIndonesia
#Result+600% organic MoM

ProblemA brand-new category with no search demand, and a site that wasn't built to answer buyers' first questions: price, financing, subsidy.
ApproachUse search intent as user research, structure the site around those questions, and give every page a path to a test ride.
#01 Context

A product people didn't know to look for

An electric motorcycle was an unfamiliar purchase for most Indonesian riders. Nobody searched for "MAKA Cavalry" yet. They searched with doubts: Is it worth it? Can I pay in installments? Is there a government subsidy? Which one is the best?

The pre-revamp website was a brand showcase. It didn't meet those questions where people asked them, and it didn't guide anyone toward a test ride or a sales conversation.

Design goal

Build a site that answers the category's first questions, earns trust for a brand-new name, and turns intent into qualified leads, all while ranking organically.

#02 Research

Search intent as user research

There was no budget or time for interviews. Instead, search data showed what thousands of potential buyers were already asking. Each keyword cluster became a user question, and each question became a page in the structure.

Price comes first

The highest-volume questions are about money (subsidy, installments), not specs. Price and financing had to be one tap from the homepage.

People compare

"Terbaik" and "terbaru" show buyers comparing options. The site had to win the comparison, not just describe one product.

Trust before the form

It's a new brand in a new category. Visitors need proof (reviews, a test ride, a familiar public figure) before they'll leave a phone number.

These three insights are my reading of the keyword data. Adjust them to what you actually concluded at the time, and add a line on the Similarweb competitor research.

#04 Process

From audit to live site

Audit

Technical SEO audit of the old site, keyword and cannibalization analysis, and competitor traffic research on Similarweb.

Structure

Grouped keywords by intent into a sitemap, then set URL structure and internal links from question pages to the product.

Design & build

Page templates designed to rank and convert, with on-page content and schema built in from day one.

Measure

Tracked visibility, rankings, and leads in GSC, GA4, and Semrush, then iterated on the pages with the most potential.

#05 Key Design Decisions

Three choices that moved the numbers

01

Navigation built on questions, not company structure

The menu and page hierarchy follow how buyers think (price, comparison, test ride), not how the company is organized.

WhyMoney and comparison questions dominate search. Hiding them in a blog would lose them.
Signal"Harga & Cicilan" sits in the always-visible rail. #1 for "motor listrik subsidi" (12.1K/mo) and "kredit motor listrik".
02

Every informational page has a next step

Articles and guides don't end at the answer. They link to Cavalry and the test-ride form, so organic traffic becomes a lead path.

WhyRanking traffic is useless if it bounces. Content needed a path into the funnel.
SignalOrganic + paid delivered 20K+ leads in six months.
03

Lead forms designed for quality, not just volume

The form and campaign landing flow were tuned so that leads arriving were ready for follow-up, not just names.

WhyFor a considered purchase, sales needs leads that are actually interested.
Signal1,000+ qualified Meta leads at under Rp10,000 per lead.

#07 What I learned
"When a category is new, the first question isn't 'what is this?', it's 'can I afford it?'"

Search data is user research at scale. It doesn't replace interviews, but it surfaces real questions from thousands of people at no cost.

SEO and UX are the same problem. A structure Google understands is usually one people can navigate.

Next time: I'd run usability tests with 5 prospective buyers on the financing page to check the installment simulation was truly easy to understand.
