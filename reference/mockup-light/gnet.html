#UX Case Study · Website from Zero

A direct line to buyers distributors used to own

Two websites built from zero so a building-materials brand could be found directly.

#PeriodAug 2023 – Jun 2024
#RolePerf. Supervisor, site lead
#MarketIndonesia
#Result13× organic clicks
ProblemAlmost no organic presence (1.25K clicks in six months). Buyers found distributors, not the brand.
ApproachBuild the brand's own sites around how buyers search for products, and keep steering content toward rising queries.
#01 Context

The brand wasn't in the conversation

When someone needs a building material, they usually ask a store or a distributor. The manufacturer's brand is invisible until the product arrives on site. Digital was GNET's chance to change that, but there was nothing for buyers to land on.

The job was to make GNET searchable and reachable directly: when a buyer looks up a product, the brand's own page should answer them.

Design goal

Give buyers a place to find the right product, understand it, and contact the brand, without going through a distributor first.

#02 Users

Who searches for building materials?

Search Console queries showed what people actually typed. These groups of searchers each needed something different from the site.

Homeowners & renovators

Searching for a product by problem or use case. Need plain explanations, photos, and a price idea.

Contractors & builders

Know what they want. Need specs, sizes, and where to buy, fast.

Stores & resellers

Looking for a supplier. Need product range and a business contact.

#04 Process

Two builds, structure to launch

Baseline

Started from 1.25K clicks and 67.5K impressions in six months: almost no organic presence.

Structure

Planned the sitemap and page templates for two websites around product categories and search demand.

Build & launch

Led both builds end to end, with content and on-page SEO in place at launch.

Steer

Used GSC and GA4 reporting to spot rising queries and point new content at them.

#05 Key Design Decisions

Three choices that moved the numbers

01

Product pages that answer the search

Each product page is written around the questions in its queries (what it is, where it fits, specs), not just a catalog photo.

WhyBuyers search with questions. A page that answers them earns the click and the trust.
SignalAverage position improved from 13.9 to 10.9.
02

Let rising queries set the content roadmap

Instead of a fixed editorial calendar, new content followed queries that were starting to grow in Search Console.

WhyDemand shows up in search data before anywhere else.
SignalImpressions grew from 67.5K to 1.06M in six months.
03

Show the product in use: video first

Meta campaigns were restructured around video creative showing the product, instead of static images.

WhyBuilding materials are easier to understand when you see them installed.
SignalCPC dropped from Rp300+ to Rp26.

#07 What I learned
"If buyers can't find you, someone else answers for you."

Owning the answer builds the brand. A product page that answers the search does more for a manufacturer than another distributor listing.

Search data is a live roadmap. Rising queries told us what to build next more reliably than guesses in a meeting.

Next time: I'd add a simple product finder ("what are you building?") to guide homeowners who don't know product names yet.
