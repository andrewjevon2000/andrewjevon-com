#UX Case Study · Conversion Journey

Replacing the form with a conversation

A chat-first journey for premium B2B buyers, where one project averages around Rp500M.

#Period2023 – 2025
#RoleDigital Marketing, journey design
#MarketIndonesia
#Result~1,300% est. ROI

ProblemHigh-value, low-volume B2B leads with long decision cycles. Generic campaigns and forms produced noise, not projects.
ApproachRoute ads straight into WhatsApp, qualify inside the conversation, and add SEO for high-intent product searches.
#01 Context

A Rp500M decision doesn't start with a form

YKK AP's buyers are architects, contractors, and project owners choosing windows, doors, and façade systems for a building. They compare options for weeks, ask detailed questions, and need to trust the people they'll work with.

A standard lead form asks them to commit before any of that trust exists. It also gives the sales team a name and a number with no context about the project.

Design goal

Design a first step that feels like talking to an expert, not filling in paperwork, and that tells sales which conversations are real projects.

#02 Insight

B2B buyers in Indonesia already decide on WhatsApp

Questions come first

Before sharing details, buyers want answers: availability, specs, rough pricing. A chat lets them ask immediately.

Conversations qualify better

A few natural questions in chat (project type, location, timeline) reveal more than any form field.

Long cycles need a thread

A WhatsApp thread stays open for weeks. Follow-up is one message away, not a cold call.

#04 Conversation Design

Designing the first message

In a chat-first journey, the conversation is the interface. Three parts mattered most:

a

A pre-filled opener

The ad opens WhatsApp with a message already written, so starting takes one tap.

b

Answer, then ask

Give the buyer something useful first, then ask about the project: type, location, timeline.

c

A clear hand-off

Qualified chats go to the right sales person with the project context already captured.

#05 Key Design Decisions

Three choices that moved the numbers

01

Chat instead of a form

Every campaign routes to WhatsApp rather than a landing-page form.

WhyFor big, slow decisions, buyers want to ask before they commit.
Signal1,800+ conversations at ~Rp20K each.
02

Qualify inside the conversation

Qualifying questions happen naturally in chat, not as mandatory fields up front.

WhyEvery extra form field costs leads. In chat, the same questions feel like service.
Signal45%+ chat conversion rate.
03

Be there when buyers search, too

An SEO layer for high-intent product queries brings in buyers who never saw an ad.

WhyArchitects research on Google long before they're ready to chat.
SignalOrganic clicks 69 → 216 and impressions 4.7K → 12.5K in two months.

#07 What I learned
"The best conversion step is the one buyers already use."

The channel is part of the UX. Moving from a form to WhatsApp changed the experience more than any landing-page redesign would have.

Qualification can feel like service. The same questions that slow down a form speed up a conversation.

Next time: I'd tag every chat by project stage in a CRM, so the journey from first message to signed project is measured end to end instead of estimated.
